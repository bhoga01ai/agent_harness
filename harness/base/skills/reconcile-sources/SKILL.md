# Reconciling sources that disagree

1. List each claim with its source, and the date of that source if the result shows one.
2. Prefer, in order: the primary source (the vendor, agency or paper itself), then the most
   recent dated source, then reporting that cites a primary source.
3. If the difference may be due to time (a newer release, an updated estimate), ask the
   searcher one focused follow-up for the latest figure and its date.
4. In the answer, give the claim you trust most first, then say briefly that other sources
   say otherwise and why you preferred this one. Keep both URLs in "Sources".
